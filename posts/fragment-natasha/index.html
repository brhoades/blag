Thinking back, I remember a night with one of my best friends, Natasha Jarus, which I drafted a note about. She was wearing new knee-high platform boots in the Seattle rain while driving us in her new (to her) 1980's truck without power brakes and an automatic transmission. She had parallel parked on a steep hill.

I had the joy of watching her attempt to reverse out in the dark on a wet street in a manual truck in platform boots while parallel parked without power brakes. She succeeded despite her self-imposed exteniuating circumstances; she's Dr. Jarus after all.

I acknowledged in the moment how ridiculous these set of constraints were. And she didn't disagree.
